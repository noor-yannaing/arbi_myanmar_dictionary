# Logo design

The app uses the Language Bridge mark: two speech shapes with an open book
between them. The design represents communication between Arabic and Myanmar.
Its navy, ivory, gold and sky-blue colors match the app palette.

The selected mark is available as [the app logo](../branding/nur-language-bridge.png).
It appears on the splash screen, search header, and Android and iOS app icons.
The other concepts are kept here for reference.
